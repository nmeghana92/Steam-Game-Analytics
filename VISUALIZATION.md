# Data Visualization and Exploratory Data Analysis

## 1. Introduction

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is performed to understand the Steam games dataset and identify useful patterns and trends.

## 2. Game Distribution Analysis

Analyzes the distribution of games available in the dataset.

## 3. Free vs Paid Games

Compares free and paid games to understand their distribution in the Steam catalog.

## 4. Genre Distribution

Visualizes the frequency of different game genres and identifies popular categories.

## 5. Game Price Analysis

Examines game prices using statistical summaries and visualizations to understand pricing patterns.

## 6. Review Sentiment Analysis

Analyzes positive and negative reviews to understand user opinions about games.

## 7. Popular Games Analysis

Identifies games with high review counts and positive user feedback.

## 8. Objective

The visualizations help users understand the Steam gaming market, explore game characteristics, and support data-driven game recommendations.
